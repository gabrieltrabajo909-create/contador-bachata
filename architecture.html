What this app deliberately does not have

Knowing what is missing explains most of the app's limits.

  • No server. A server is a computer that stays on somewhere and answers requests. This app has none, so there is nothing to pay for, nothing to maintain — and no way for two phones to talk to each other.
  • No accounts. Nobody logs in, so nothing is tied to a person.
  • No shared library. Songs a teacher records stay on the teacher's phone. Getting them to a student means exporting a file and importing it on the other device, by hand.
  • No control over the music. The song plays on Spotify or a speaker; the app only listens. That is why it cannot slow a track down or loop a section.
  • No audio is stored. Only a mathematical summary of the sound is kept, which cannot be turned back into music.
